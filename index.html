Investment Management Firm

GMS Capital Partners LLC

An investment management firm focused on sourcing, arranging and making investments across different asset classes, including private equity, venture capital, real estate and other alternative asset classes. Established in 2017, the firm is incorporated in Delaware and manages several investment vehicles on behalf of its parent company, GMS Holdings.

GMS Holdings is a family owned holding company for a globally diversified group of companies and assets across a spectrum of industries including life sciences, telecommunications, specialty contracting, energy and agri sciences.

Private Equity

GMS Capital Partners’ private equity team is focused on making private equity investments in growth-oriented, high-potential middle market companies, primarily in North America. We create value by bringing world-class strategic, operational and financial resources of our global network to our portfolio companies. We partner and align with management teams to enhance value for our investors and managers.

Investment Criteria

Industry Focus

We target strategic platforms like life sciences, telecom, and energy, alongside resilient, sector-agnostic assets. Our focus is on cash generative, asset-backed companies with clear organic or M&A growth potential.

Hold Period

Ability to hold assets on a long-term basis without the need or pressure to exit.

Ownership & Activism

Depending on nature of investment (passive for fund investment; potential for influence in other VC/Venture Building investments.

Transaction Size

Ability to execute larger transactions through our portfolio companies or with our partners.

Management / Partner

Preference for having a partner and/or management team identified, especially in “Opportunistic” transactions where we don’t have the support of a strategic platform.

Transaction Type

Buyouts, growth equity, carve-outs, real assets. Will consider debt/mezzanine investments that offer equity-like returns.

Venture Capital

We power up exceptional entrepreneurs that are trying to use technology to solve real problems, accelerating positive change and exponential transformation. As previous entrepreneurs who have founded, invested and scaled multi-million dollar businesses, we have assembled the ultimate founder-centric platform — one that provides our investees not just with capital but also with resources and expertise to support every phase of the company’s life cycle.

Investment Criteria

Sector Focus

We invest in all types of software technology sectors, consumer and enterprise.

Geography Focus

Venture building: MENA. Direct investments: MENA, or global with a MENA strategy. Fund investments: ex-MENA.

Approach

Direct investments — venture capital, growth equity, venture debt; we can design a bespoke financing solution that fits the stage and type of financing. Venture building — building investable, scalable and sustainable businesses from scratch. Fund investments — LP in early-stage VC funds with co-investment rights.

Stage Focus

We invest in all stages: Seed, Series A, Series B and Growth. We may lead and/or co-invest in a financing round.

Hold Period

We are time agnostic with no pressure or need to exit. We know it takes time to build a world-class company and we are your partner for as long as it takes.

Ownership & Activism

Depending on nature of investment (passive for fund investment; potential for influence in other VC/Venture Building investments.
